About

I’ve been a serial portfolio careerist most of my life. What that means is that I haven’t worked one job at a time or stuck to one well-defined career path. Instead, I’ve always invested in a portfolio of projects at the same time. That’s how I’ve been a product strategist, business consultant, adjunct teacher, and poet depending to whom I talked!

But there are recurring motifs:

Creative Writing

This is my zone of genius—the thing I do so easily that I don’t even know I’ve done it. I imagine the thing that’s missing, then I write it into being no matter the medium.

Teaching

If I can teach it, then I know it. If I know it, then I can teach it to anybody—no matter if they’re a student in Japan or the cross-functional team at a global brand.

Human-centered/User Experience Design

When I learned the design process, I finally had words for something I always did instinctively. It’s what keeps my work focused on people.

Product Building

Stories, poems, books, websites, lesson plans, apps, platforms, operational systems, roadmaps, etc.—they are all just products.

Strategic Consulting

How do you get from here to there? That’s the whole game, and the best ones ripple past the boardroom and keep running even after I’m gone.

Entrepreneurship

Did you know the word means someone who assumes all the risk? That’s what art is. Or being a teacher. Or running a business. Or taking a leap into unknown territory.
