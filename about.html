About

Here’s what the 🤖 says about me as of July 31, 2026 with strikethroughs and edits by me:

“Sarah Dzida is a UX and product strategy consultant with over 15 years of experience turning complex, messy ideas into clear digital experiences scalable solutions. Working as a fractional UX and product leader, she advises B2B and B2B2C teams on roadmaps, websites, and product innovation, and has partnered with organizations including Walmart, Toyota, the City of Los Angeles, and the Bruce Lee Foundation.

Her work has earned industry recognition, including honors at the Creativity International Awards for a Lexus project and at the Communication Arts Interaction Awards for interactive storytelling. Beyond client work, she leads design-thinking workshops and has mentored UX practitioners and professionals across skill levels.

Sarah is also a working writer. Her essays on design and strategy have appeared in UX Booth, UX Collective, and UX Magazine, and are used in UX curricula nationally. She publishes a newsletter, Sarah’s Notebook, on creativity and transformation, and is the author of Dearest Enemy, a hybrid poetry memoir about friendship and Japan.

Based in Los Angeles, she moves fluidly between strategy and craft — a writer’s eye for structure paired with a strategist’s rigor for story.”
