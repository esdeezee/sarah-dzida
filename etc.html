Awards

Award of Merit — Travis County Civil & Family Courts

Issued by ENR Texas & Southwest · 2023

Three separate judging panels collectively reviewed 100 entries, examining factors such as safety, innovation, craft quality, and community benefit. I worked with Gensler on the connected digital experiences for this $300M courthouse in downtown Austin, Texas.

Best Interactive Content Piece or Series — KidHQ

Issued by Digiday · 2020

Winners utilize emerging channels and cutting-edge technology to tell stories, formulate strategies and forge partnerships. KidHQ was a multi-floor virtual toy story and branded experience with 8M interactions. I worked with Technology Humans & Taste, eko, and Buzzfeed on this Walmart experience.

Best Interactive Mobile/Tablet Entertainment — Whaddyawannaknow

Issued by CommArts Magazine · 2020

CommArts Magazines annually recognizes the best work being done in visual communications. This was an interactive branded experience for Walmart that included 2.5 hours of unique gameplay and a 30-ft long user journey. Partners: Technology Humans & Taste, eko, Particle3

2x Silver Telly Winner for Interactive Video & Branded Content — KidHQ

Issued by The Telly Awards · 2020

The Telly Awards honor excellence in video and television across all screens. KidHQ invited kids to explore branded Walmart & Mattel experiences through their smartphones/tablets, then share wish lists with their parents for the holidays—over 139,000 received!

2x Platinum Winner for Website & Digital Magazine Design — See Lexus

Issued by Creativity International Awards · 2018

One of the longest-running, independent advertising and graphic design competition, See Lexus was a cohesive digital hub that showcases the expansive creativity of the brand—from yachts to hoverboards and chefs to poetry. Partner: Five & Done
