30 years ago, DTS was the audio pioneer for movie theaters and a consumer-facing brand. But they had actually evolved into a B2B business—selling to OEMs. The most important work I did was updating DTS’s understanding of their own brand, products, and services—and how they were both distinct from and connected to the larger Xperi brand.
